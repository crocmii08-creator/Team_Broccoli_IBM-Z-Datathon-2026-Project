# Kaki accessible explainer

Confirmed concept: follow Ah Ma's phone through guided booking, a scam message, and a scam call.

Deliverable: a local upload package for YouTube or Vimeo, under three minutes. English narration; readable burned-in English captions; equivalent SRT and WebVTT; a descriptive transcript; a thumbnail; suggested title, description, and upload instructions. No publishing was requested in this production stage.

Visual direction: Kaki's teal and yellow on a warm, light background; deep teal text; a persistent phone and the expressive line companion. The camera moves between the phone and details rather than replacing unrelated title cards. Pauses allow viewers to read. No flashing or rapid montage. Large captions occupy a reserved lower strip.

Continuity: the same phone carries the story. The home screen opens into HealthHub; its confirmation returns to the phone and gives way to WhatsApp; the warning sheet leads to family support; the same device receives the scam call. The companion and phone survive the feature recap and final lockup.

Evidence: UI strings, illustrative scam message, call transcript, and classifier outputs are extracted from the current working `kaki-demo.html` and `kaki_model.json`. The movie is a deterministic, legibility-adapted UI reconstruction. Simulated calls, messages, bookings, and family alerts remain labelled. IBM LinuxONE is described as the deployment target, not a verified live deployment. Impact is an intended outcome, not a measured claim.

Reference: OneTake's bundled `cases/skill-demo-15s/skill-demo.mp4`, used for motion analysis, not for shipping footage. The selected narrated demonstration intentionally uses longer reading holds and gentler camera movement.

Narration: audition available Microsoft English voices. The bundled Kokoro model is not installed; use an available speech engine while retaining line-based timings and timed subtitles.
